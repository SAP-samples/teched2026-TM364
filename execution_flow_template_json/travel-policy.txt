# Corporate Travel Policy — Europe & USA

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Owner:** Finance & Controlling

---

## 1. General Rules

- All business travel must have a valid business purpose (customer visit, internal event, conference, or training).
- Trips must be approved by the employee's line manager **before** booking.
- All expenses must be submitted within **14 days** after the trip ends.
- Receipts are mandatory for any single expense above **€/$ 25**.

---

## 2. Flights

### Europe
| Flight Duration | Permitted Class |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Up to 3 hours  | Economy only    |
| 3–6 hours      | Economy only    |
| Over 6 hours   | Business class permitted |

### USA
| Flight Duration | Permitted Class |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Up to 5 hours  | Economy only    |
| Over 5 hours   | Business class permitted |

> Upgrades using personal frequent flyer miles are permitted and do not require approval.

---

## 3. Hotels

### Europe
| City Category     | Maximum Rate per Night (incl. taxes) |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Standard cities   | € 180                                |
| High-cost cities (Paris, London, Zurich, Amsterdam) | € 250 |
| All other cities  | € 160                                |

### USA
| City Category     | Maximum Rate per Night (incl. taxes) |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Standard cities   | $ 200                                |
| High-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston) | $ 300 |
| All other cities  | $ 180                                |

> Hotels must be booked via the corporate booking tool where possible.

---

## 4. Meals & Dining

### Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem)

| Region          | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Full Day |
|-----------------|-----------|-------|--------|----------|
| Europe          | € 15      | € 25  | € 40   | € 80     |
| USA             | $ 20      | $ 30  | $ 50   | $ 100    |

### Customer Entertainment (Business Meals)
- **Customer visits:** Up to € 80 / $ 100 per person (including employee).
- **Internal team meals:** Up to € 40 / $ 50 per person.
- Alcohol expenses are reimbursable up to **€/$ 20 per person** and only for customer entertainment.
- Receipts must include the names and company affiliations of all attendees.

---

## 5. Ground Transportation

### Taxi & Ride-Sharing
- Permitted for airport transfers and when public transport is not reasonably available.
- Maximum: € 60 / $ 80 per single ride within city limits.
- Airport transfers above this limit require a receipt and brief justification.

### Rental Cars
- Economy or compact class only.
- SUV or premium class requires manager approval.
- GPS add-ons are reimbursable. Additional insurance is reimbursable only outside the home country.

### Public Transport
- Fully reimbursable with receipt.
- Rail travel in Europe: Standard/2nd class for journeys up to 4 hours; 1st class permitted for journeys over 4 hours.

---

## 6. Trip Categories & Approval Rules

| Category           | Pre-approval Required | Expense Limit (per trip) |
|--------------------|-----------------------|--------------------------|
| Customer Visit     | Manager               | No cap                   |
| Internal Event     | Manager               | € / $ 500 per day        |
| Conference / TechEd| Manager + HR          | No cap (with invite)     |
| Training           | Manager + HR          | No cap (with enrollment) |
| Partner Event      | Manager               | No cap                   |

---

## 7. Out-of-Policy Expenses

Expenses exceeding the limits defined in this policy are not automatically reimbursed.
The employee must submit an **exception request** including:

1. The expense amount and category
2. Justification for exceeding the limit
3. Manager approval (email confirmation acceptable)

Exception requests are reviewed by Finance within **5 business days**.

---

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are **not** reimbursed under any circumstances:

- Personal leisure activities (tours, cinema, sporting events)
- Minibar charges
- In-flight Wi-Fi (unless required for urgent business — requires justification)
- Fines, penalties, or traffic violations
- Clothing or personal items
- Spouse / partner travel costs
- Expenses without receipt (above € / $ 25)
